## Test Case: TC-004

### Test Name
Player Elimination During Voting Phase

### Test Objective
Verify that during the voting phase, when a player is selected for elimination, they are displayed in grayscale.

### Preconditions
- A game is in progress and has reached the voting phase.

### Steps to Execute
1. Start a new game from the Home screen.
2. Nominate at least one player for elimination.
3. Progress through the game to reach the voting phase.
5. Confirm the elimination.

### Expected Result
- The selected player is displayed in grayscale, indicating elimination.

### Actual Result
- Selected player is eliminated, displayed in grayscale
